How to Delete Your Account and Data
At Contact Cleaner, we give you full control over your personal information. If you wish to delete your account and all associated data, please follow these simple steps:

Open the App: Launch Contact Cleaner on your mobile device.
Go to Settings: Tap the Profile Picture on the main dashboard.
Find Account Section: Scroll down to the "Account" or "Personal Information" section.
Delete Account: Tap on the "Delete Account" button.
Confirm Deletion: A confirmation dialog will appear warning you that this action is permanent. Tap "Delete" to confirm.
What happens when you delete your account?

Cloud Backups: All your contact backups stored on our Google Firebase servers will be immediately and permanently erased.
Metadata: Your user profile, email association, and app settings will be deleted.
No Recovery: This action cannot be undone. Once deleted, your data cannot be recovered.
Note: Deleting the app from your phone does not automatically delete your cloud data. You must use the "Delete Account" feature within the app to ensure all server-side data is removed.
